## Personal Training in a Private Studio
One-on-one and semi-private coaching in our private home studio in North Austin, booked to one person or one small group at a time. No membership and no waiting for a rack. The same two coaches who have run Outright since 2009.
Already train with us? Book your next session: https://outrightfitness.com/book-personal-training/

## How This Works
Step 1, Tell Us What You Want: your goal, your training history, anything that hurts, and when you can train.
Step 2, We Recommend a Starting Point: a coach replies within 1-2 business days with the format that fits.
Step 3, Book and Train: choose your sessions and pick your times.

## Pay What You Can
Studio training carries a regular rate and a minimum. You choose any amount at or above the minimum when you book. This applies to studio sessions only. Training at your location, private pods, team training and performance testing are quoted at fixed rates.

## Semi-Private
Capped at three, everyone is on their own program. Lower cost per person.

## Teen Strength & Power
Strength and power for athletes 14 and up, in the studio, three at a time. Every athlete under 18 needs a parent or guardian to sign the waiver before the first session, and parents are always welcome to stay.

## Where You'll Train
A private, purpose-built training space in North Austin: a strength room with racks, bars, dumbbells, plus a climate-controlled room for conditioning, mobility, and assessment work. This is a converted garage and office at our home, not a commercial gym. Parking is on the street directly outside. You will get the exact address and arrival instructions once your first session is booked.

## Nutrition Comes With It
Every personal training client gets nutrition guidance and accountability included. One structured check-in each week and a longer review once a month. You will hear back within one business day. We are not dietitians and we do not provide medical nutrition therapy.

## Youth & Teen Groups
Outdoors is where speed and agility get trained. Your first outdoor group session is free.
