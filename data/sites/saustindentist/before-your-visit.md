## Becoming a New Patient
Please call 512-280-1117 to begin the process of becoming a new patient. Our friendly and helpful staff will determine your needs and get you scheduled. We update health history every two years and prefer that it is provided to us prior to your appointment. You can submit your information online at https://www.saustindentist.com/medical-history-form

## What to Expect
Your doctor will listen to your concerns, perform an exam, review x-rays and intra-oral photos, then discuss your dental needs. A treatment plan will be presented. If time permits, treatment may be performed at this initial appointment.

## Changing Your Scheduled Appointment
If you find the need to change your scheduled appointment, please allow two working days notice whenever possible. Repeat violations of this policy will result in a cancellation fee starting at $50 depending on the length of the appointment.

## Payment Options
If you have dental insurance, please bring your insurance card and ID with you to your first appointment. Cash or check payments are due at the time services are rendered. We also accept Visa, American Express, MasterCard, Discover and CareCredit. As a courtesy, we will bill your insurance directly for services provided. We are not providers for any HMO, Medicare or Medicaid dental policies. Senior discounts are available for patients over 65.

## In Network Insurance Plans
Please call or email us to determine if we are currently in network with your insurance plan.

## Services
Dr Shelby Knight and his sister, Dr Miranda Torrey, offer services in family dentistry including Invisalign, whitening, crowns, fillings, root canals, dentures, partial dentures, implants, porcelain veneers, bridges, and extractions.
