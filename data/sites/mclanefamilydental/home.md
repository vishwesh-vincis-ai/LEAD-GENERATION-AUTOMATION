## Meet the Doctors
Dr. Deidra was born and raised in Lubbock, Texas. She graduated from the top ranked UT Health Science Center at San Antonio Dental School in 2007.
Dr. James graduated Summa Cum Laude from Texas A&M University before attending the UT Health Science Center at San Antonio Dental School, graduating in 2007.
Dr. Zachary Carlson attended Texas A&M Health Science Center Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas. He has been providing patient-tailored dental care in south Austin since 2018. His favorite aspects of dentistry are forming authentic lasting connections with patients and helping alleviate the concerns of the dentally anxious.
