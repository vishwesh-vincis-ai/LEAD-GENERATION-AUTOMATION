## Get Started
A member of our team will reach out to help you set up your FREE consultation and assessment. Complete the form below to schedule a session with a personal trainer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get started with personal training at Austin Simply Fit?
Fill out the contact form on the Get Started page with your name, email, and phone number. A member of the ASF team will reach out within 24 hours (one business day) to schedule your free one-hour consultation and structural balance assessment. There's no commitment required — the consultation is completely complimentary.

### What is included in the free consultation at Austin Simply Fit?
Your free consultation is a full hour and has two parts. The first part is a conversation with your personal trainer covering your health and fitness goals, medical history, and training background, plus a walkthrough of ASF's training programs, packages, and studio policies. The second part is a hands-on structural balance assessment where your trainer evaluates your movement quality and fitness level. You also receive a free body composition analysis using ASF's InBody machine.

### How long does it take to hear back after submitting the Get Started form?
ASF aims to respond within 24 hours (one business day) of receiving your form submission. You can also call or email either studio location, ASF Central on Burnet Rd or ASF South on South Lamar.

### What should I bring or do to prepare for my first visit?
After your consultation is scheduled, you will receive an intake form to complete before meeting with your trainer. For your consultation, wear comfortable workout clothes and athletic shoes. After your consultation, you'll also receive ASF's Wellness Packet with lifestyle and recovery recommendations.

### What happens after the free consultation? Am I locked into anything?
No. If you decide to move forward, you and your trainer will find a mutually convenient schedule and book your ongoing training sessions. There are no memberships or long-term contracts. Sessions are purchased in packages of 5, 10, or 20.

### Can I request a specific trainer at Austin Simply Fit?
You can share preferences, and ASF will do their best to accommodate. Trainer matching is primarily based on schedule availability and fit for your specific goals. Some trainers specialize in athletic development, injury rehabilitation, or pre- and postnatal exercise, but all ASF trainers complete the same rigorous 13-week onboarding program.

### How is Austin Simply Fit different from signing up at a commercial gym?
Every session is by appointment with a dedicated certified trainer who has designed a program specifically for you. There are no crowds, no waiting for equipment, and no generic workouts. ASF operates as a private personal training studio, built around focused 30-minute sessions.

## Personal Training Programs
One-on-One Personal Training: private, one-on-one 30-minute training sessions with a Certified Personal Trainer.
Multi-Client Personal Training: semi-private, two or three clients train together in 30-minute sessions with a Certified Personal Trainer.
We also offer virtual training to keep you consistent at home or while traveling. Nutrition and health coaching through THRIVE with Keyspan at Austin Simply Fit.
