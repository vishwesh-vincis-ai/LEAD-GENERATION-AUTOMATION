We audited 11 independent dental practices and fitness coaches in Austin, TX, threw out what the model invented, re-checked each gap on deeper pages, and loaded the top three into their own demo bot on the NVIDIA-backed Business Brain. Why the checks exist

What the first run caught

  • coolcreekfamilydental.com: HTTP 403: page not actually read. The model still returned “Example Business Name”.
  • 3 model claims overruled by the links. “Make appointment” went to a contact page, not a scheduler. “Short call” is not a texting option. “Pay what you can” is not a price list.
  • Homepages lie by omission. Outright Fitness looked like “no prices, request form only”. Its training page lists $125 drop-ins and sends buyers straight to the scheduler. Both gaps were dropped and the ranking changed: South Austin Family Dental (#4 → #3), Austin Simply Fit (#5 → #4), Outright Fitness (#3 → #5). An email built on the homepage alone would have been wrong.

The one miss

The bot had McLane's hours and still said it didn't know

The McLane page has an Office Schedule section:

Mon. 8am – 5pm. Tue. 8am – 5pm. Wed. 8am – 5pm. Thu. 8am – 5pm. Fri. Closed. Sat. Closed. Sun. Closed.

The question “Are you open on Fridays?” should get “No, we're closed on Fridays.” Instead the bot handed off. We traced it to retrieval, not the model. Keyword search can't connect “Fridays” or “hours” to “Fri.” and “Office Schedule”, so it returns nothing. Vector search ranks the schedule first, but its similarity sits just under the brain's 0.30 cutoff, so it is discarded.

QuestionSchedule chunkNext best chunkCutoffResult
Are you open on Fridays?0.2820.1020.30Filtered out
What are your hours?0.2890.1540.30Filtered out

Why it matters: McLane's outreach draft promises an assistant that “already knows your site, down to your hours”. That claim fails on the second question. The draft cannot go out until this is fixed.

What fixes it

  1. At ingest, expand day abbreviations (“Fri.” → “Friday”) and add “hours” to schedule headings, so keyword search catches it. Low risk, and it helps every tenant.
  2. Add hours questions to the brain's eval set before touching MIN_VECTOR_SIM. The brain's own evals show answerable and unanswerable similarities overlap, so lowering the cutoff blindly trades this miss for false answers.
  3. Re-run python -m leadengine.demo and rebuild this page. The grade for this turn should flip to Correct.
